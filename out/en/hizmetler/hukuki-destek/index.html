Legal Support

Overview

From the NIE application to the title transfer, from taxes to the contract, the whole process is managed transparently; we don't provide legal services ourselves — we connect you with trusted partners.

Process

1

Initial Meeting

Needs and timeline are assessed.

2

NIE Application

We guide you through obtaining your Foreigner Identity Number.

3

Due Diligence

Land registry and debt checks are carried out.

4

Notary & Title Transfer

Signing and ownership transfer are completed.

Who We Work With

We work with Valencia-based partner lawyers specialized in real estate law who communicate in Turkish or English.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I buy without an NIE?

No, an NIE is mandatory; the process usually takes 1-4 weeks.

Who pays the lawyer's fee?

The buyer does, typically around 1-1.5% of the property price.
